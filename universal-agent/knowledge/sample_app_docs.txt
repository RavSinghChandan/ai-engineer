FAQ:
Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes, all tiers support CSV and JSON export from Settings > Export.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: iOS and Android apps are available. Search "My App" in the app store.

Q: How do I invite team members?
A: Go to Settings > Team > Invite Member and enter their email address.
