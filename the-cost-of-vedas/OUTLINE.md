# The Cost of the Vedas
### Fourteen dimensions of modern human life, weighed against an inheritance

**Author:** Chandan Kumar

## The frame

Not fourteen topics inside the Vedas. **Fourteen dimensions of a life being
lived right now** — the things a modern human actually has to manage — each
asked the same question:

> What did this inheritance give this part of my life, and what did it cost?

Every chapter ends in a SWOT, so the reader can see both sides at a glance
rather than being told which way to feel.

## Chapter structure

1. **Opening** — a concrete modern scene, not a scriptural throat-clear
2. **What the texts actually say** — cited by hymn/verse, limits marked
3. **The gift** — stated plainly, not inflated
4. **The cost** — stated plainly, not softened
5. **SWOT** — the dimension at a glance
6. **What remains** — where this stands today, diaspora included

## Closing

**A Personal Reckoning** — in the author's voice. Written last.

## Citation rules

1. Hymns cited by number so any reader can check any edition.
2. Attribution (Anukramaṇī) never confused with authorship.
3. Where scholars disagree, say so and take no side.
4. Interpretation labelled as interpretation.
