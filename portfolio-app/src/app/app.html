The Person Behind the Code

From Backend Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer

I started where real systems live — Java, Spring Boot, APIs, databases and Kafka, at aviation and banking scale. That taught me how enterprise software actually runs, and where it breaks.

Then I learned AI deeply and saw the gap: demos are easy, production is not. So I build the whole thing — I scope the problem, build the agents and the interface, wire it into what already exists, harden it, ship it and run it. Aura with Rav is live at aurawithrav.com and I own it end to end. At Deloitte USI I bring the same approach to agentic AI and security.

The goal is simple: be the engineer a team trusts to turn a messy problem into a working system.
